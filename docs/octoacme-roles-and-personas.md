# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads own the strategic direction and success of assigned product areas. They drive prioritization decisions, stakeholder alignment, and measure delivery against business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Approve go/no-go decisions at initiation and planning gates
- Review and validate success metrics defined by Product Managers
- Escalate risks and dependencies that impact product strategy
- Align with Sponsors and cross-functional stakeholders
- Review retrospective outcomes and approve process improvements

### Goals
- Ensure product initiatives deliver measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across multiple concurrent projects
- Enable clear decision-making and reduce escalation cycles

### Typical Communication
- Weekly sync with Project Manager and Product Manager
- Stakeholder briefings and decision gate reviews
- Escalation communication for business-impacting risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Sets the strategic context that Product Managers translate into roadmap priorities and acceptance criteria
- Reviews Project Managers' status and risk reports at decision gates to approve continued investment
- Provides Developers with business rationale when trade-off decisions are needed during planning

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality standards, testing strategy, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality bar before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing approach and QA strategy during planning
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria validation
- Perform manual QA and coordinate automation testing
- Identify quality risks and blockers
- Validate completion of Definition of Done before release

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects reaching production
- Reduce manual testing effort through automation
- Build confidence in product readiness through comprehensive validation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- PR reviews (QA sign-off on acceptance criteria)
- Pre-release smoke test coordination
- Quality metrics and defect trending in weekly syncs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers during implementation and code review to confirm acceptance criteria are testable and met
- Works with Product Managers to translate success metrics into testable acceptance criteria
- Reports quality risks and readiness status to Project Managers ahead of release decision gates

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide funding, strategic alignment, and executive authority for projects. They represent business and customer interests at the leadership level.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case during initiation
- Provide strategic guidance and business context
- Serve as final escalation authority for critical blockers
- Review and approve major scope or timeline changes
- Receive monthly status updates and participate in key decision gates

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value aligned with strategy
- Minimize business disruption from project issues
- Maintain visibility and control over high-investment initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Decision gate reviews (initiation, planning, release approval)
- Escalation paths for business-critical issues
- Post-project retrospectives and lessons learned

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Project Managers with the escalation path of last resort for business-critical blockers
- Approves the business case and scope changes that Product Managers use to guide prioritization
- Relies on Project Managers and Product Managers for accurate, timely status reporting ahead of decision gates

---

## Tech Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads/Architects own technical design decisions, system architecture, and technical risk mitigation. They guide the team toward sound, scalable technical solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and review technical architecture and design approaches
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Guide technology and tooling choices during planning
- Review complex pull requests and technical design docs
- Mentor Developers on best practices and technical standards

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and secure
- Reduce technical debt and architectural risk
- Enable consistent technical decision-making across the team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design review sessions
- Technical design docs and RFC-style proposals
- Technical risk updates in planning and risk reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to guide implementation approach and review technical designs
- Advises Project Managers on technical risks and dependencies that affect timelines
- Collaborates with Product Managers to assess technical feasibility and trade-offs during planning

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads own security compliance, risk assessment, and incident response. They ensure the team builds and operates software that meets security and compliance standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and features for security risks during planning
- Define and enforce security standards and compliance requirements
- Lead incident response for security-related issues
- Perform or coordinate security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Approve release readiness from a security standpoint

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and compliance gaps
- Ensure rapid, effective response to security incidents
- Build a culture of security awareness across the team

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and pre-release gates
- Incident response coordination and post-incident reviews
- Security risk updates in risk registers and status reports

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to review code and designs for security vulnerabilities
- Informs Project Managers of security risks and incident status for risk registers and escalation
- Aligns with Product Managers on compliance requirements that affect feature scope

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Delivery Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach the team on effective delivery practices. They focus on team health and process improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove blockers impacting team velocity
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track team metrics (velocity, cycle time) and surface trends
- Foster collaboration and healthy team dynamics

### Goals
- Maximize team throughput and predictability
- Continuously improve team processes and practices
- Maintain a healthy, collaborative team environment

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Blocker escalation to Project Managers or leads as needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Facilitates ceremonies that Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers participate in
- Surfaces blockers to Project Managers that require cross-team coordination or escalation
- Partners with Project Managers on process improvements identified in retrospectives
